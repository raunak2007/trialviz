Every bar, point, node, and edge can be clicked to see the trial records behind it, or use the table under the chart. ⌘ Enter or Ctrl Enter in the question box runs it.

Ask a question to get started

Questions can be about trends over time, how trials are distributed across phases, statuses, sponsors or countries, comparisons between drugs or conditions, networks of sponsors and drugs, and scatter plots or histograms of enrollment and duration.

Numbers come from the registry's records or its own counts, never from the language model, and the response says whether each value is exact or sampled.
